## Observation

A cable bends, gets pulled and gets sat on. On a monitor with a fixed cable, one broken wire ends the life of the whole product.

## Position

The cable should be a replaceable part. If it fails, you swap the cable and keep the monitors. It also lets you choose a different length, or a cable with a microphone, without buying new earpieces.

## Cost

A removable cable needs a connector on each earpiece. That adds a little size, a little weight, and one more joint that can wear. We think the trade is worth it for a product meant to be kept.

## Decided

- The Angostura Uno is being designed around a detachable cable.
- The connector is 0.78 mm 2-pin: common, inexpensive to replace, and easy to find cables for.

## Open
