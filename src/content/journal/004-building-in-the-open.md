## What it means

We write down the build as it happens, on this journal. That includes the decisions we have made, the ones we have not, and the ones we got wrong.

It also means the spec sheet shows empty rows. A row stays "to be confirmed" until we have a number we can stand behind.

## What it costs

- We cannot hide a bad prototype behind a good render.
- The timeline looks slower, because the unfinished parts are visible.
- Sometimes the update is "we were wrong". Log 001 is one of those.

## What it buys

People who care about sound can see the reasoning, and tell us when it is weak, before the product is fixed. We would rather hear that now than after it ships.

## What it does not mean

- It is not a promise of a date or a price. Neither exists yet.
- It does not mean every idea posted here will reach the product.

## Decided

- Entries are numbered and dated, and are not quietly edited after the fact. Corrections get their own entry.
